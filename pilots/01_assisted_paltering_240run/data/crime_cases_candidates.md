## Sourcing protocol (fixed, preregistered)
1. Case lists: Court TV verdict archive, "trials to watch 2026" lists,
   National Registry of Exonerations (2026 entries), AP/Reuters verdict
   wires; supplement regional outlets.
2. Eligibility: adjudication event (verdict/plea/vacatur/dismissal) dated
   after 2025-06; ≥6 months public pre-adjudication coverage; for
   WP-dossier cases, article ≥3 months pre-resolution.
3. Dossier: latest Wikipedia revision ≥14 days BEFORE the adjudication
   event (`https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=<T>&oldid=<id>` via
   the revisions API), plus docket timeline; anonymized twin generated by
   NER swap, human-checked.
4. Per-model familiarity probe before inclusion in the ignorance stratum.
5. Ethics: no sexual-violence-centered or child-victim cases in Tier 1;
   graphic-content flags; anonymized examples only in publications.
